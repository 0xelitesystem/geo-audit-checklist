# Playbook: Local business

Tuning the full GEO checklist for a local service business (restaurant, dentist, lawyer, plumber, etc).

## Highest-impact items

1. **LocalBusiness schema with full address, geo, openingHours, priceRange.** AI engines surface local results from this.
2. **Service pages for every service you offer.** One page per service, with FAQPage schema for common questions about that service.
3. **Google Business Profile completely filled out, weekly posts, photo uploads.** Outside your site but AI engines pull from GBP heavily.
4. **Reviews on Google, Yelp, and one industry-specific platform.** Real reviews; never fake.
5. **Location-specific landing pages** if you serve multiple cities (one page per city, not one page covering all).

## Lower priority for local business

- llms.txt (still helpful but the local schema matters more)
- SoftwareApplication schema (skip)
- Person schema for staff (only if staff are public-facing experts)

## Local-specific paragraphs

- Homepage: city, service, years in business, and one specific outcome in the first sentence
- Service pages: what's included, what's not, typical price range, typical timeline
- Contact page: hours, address, parking, public transit access
- About page: real photo of owner, years in the community, one specific local affiliation

## Local-specific E-E-A-T

- Owner's name and photo on the About page
- Owner has a LinkedIn linked from the About page (sameAs)
- License numbers (where applicable) visible on the site
- Real customer reviews with first names and city

## Common local business mistakes

- Address only in the footer image (alt text), not as text
- Hours not updated when seasonally changed
- One page covering all 8 cities you serve (cannibalization)
- Stock photos of generic offices instead of your actual location
- No FAQs answering the questions your phone receptionist hears all day
