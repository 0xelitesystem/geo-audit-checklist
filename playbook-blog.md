# Playbook: Editorial blog

Tuning the full GEO checklist for an editorial/blog site (publishing 2+ posts a week, monetized by ads, affiliate, or subscriptions).

## Highest-impact items

1. **Author Person schema for every writer with strong sameAs.** Editorial trust collapses without it.
2. **Article schema with datePublished AND dateModified on every post.**
3. **Topical clusters with pillar pages.** Random topics get ignored; depth-on-one-thing gets cited.
4. **Quote-able first paragraph in every post.** Lead with the answer, not the setup.
5. **Visible byline at top of every post, not in the footer.**

## Lower priority

- LocalBusiness schema (skip)
- SoftwareApplication schema (only on tool reviews)
- llms.txt (helpful but article schema matters more)

## Blog-specific paragraphs

- Post intros: state the claim in sentence one, evidence in sentence two
- Section openers under every H2: answer the section question in the first paragraph
- Conclusions: one paragraph summarizing the 3 to 5 specific takeaways
- About-author sidebar: photo, 2-sentence bio, link to author page

## Blog-specific E-E-A-T

- Every writer has an author page with bio, photo, sameAs, post archive
- "Edited by" credit on news pieces
- "Fact-checked by" credit on data-heavy pieces
- "Last reviewed" date on evergreen content reviewed quarterly

## Common blog mistakes

- "Posted by Admin" on every article
- 500 posts, no topical structure, scattered across 30 topics
- Posts open with throat-clearing ("In today's world...") instead of the claim
- Updates change a typo and bump dateModified, signaling fake freshness
- Image-heavy posts with no transcript-worthy prose
